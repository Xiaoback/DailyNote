KEEP YOUR NEXT STEP CLOSE

A quick glance.
Then back to work.

With the app running, a roughly 0.4-second dwell in the top-right corner opens the panel. It stays visible while the pointer is inside, then saves and hides shortly after exit.

MAKE THE NEXT STEP CLEAR

Break work down.
Watch progress add up.

Use Tab and Shift+Tab to change nesting one level at a time. Fold any branch to focus on what matters. Moving a parent moves its descendants too.

Complete every descendant task and its task ancestors become complete automatically. Reopen a descendant and completed ancestors reopen.

Cancelled tasks do not count as complete. Indenting requires a preceding item at a suitable parent level.

KEEP YOUR OWN RHYTHM

A familiar plan.
A fresh day.

Save groups, nested tasks and rich formatting as your own daily templates, or create a template from the current note.

Choose a date and insert a template manually. It appends to that note, resets task states to pending and supports undo.

A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Do I need Xcode or a developer account?

Using the prebuilt DMG requires neither. Building from source requires full Xcode and command-line tools.

Where are notes stored?

Notes, templates, settings and automatic backups are stored locally. Export and backup-import controls are available in the app menu.

~/Library/Application Support/DailyNote/

When does the hot corner work?

While the app is running. Toggle it in the app menu. Quitting with Command-Q stops the trigger. Automatic login launch is not configured.

Can it sync to my phone or a team?

The current release supports local notes and manual backup migration. Cloud sync, mobile apps and team collaboration are not available. The public app does not include a Notification Center widget.

Where can I read the source and product facts?

Find the MIT-licensed source on GitHub. The WenKai font is licensed under SIL OFL 1.1. Structured product facts summarize capabilities and current limitations.
